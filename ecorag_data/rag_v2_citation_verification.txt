------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER (unchanged):
The main challenges in modernising electricity grids include:

1. **Electricity Demand Growth**: Electricity demand is rising rapidly, leading to increased strain on existing grid infrastructure. According to Document [2], where the grid cannot keep pace, the consequences are well documented, including lengthening connection queues, competition among different types of energy sources, and risks of service disruptions.

2. **Thermal Limits and System Strength**: Traditional grid design relies on thermal limits to manage flow within the network. However, as converter-connected resources displace synchronous generators, some stabilizing properties previously provided by synchronous machines, such as inertia, voltage support, fault current contribution, and damping, become less reliable. This raises the importance of high-quality data and validated models for managing these variables effectively.

3. **Digital Tools and AI Applications**: Digitalization and the integration of advanced technologies like AI are crucial for capturing untapped capacity in existing networks. Document [3] highlights that distributed energy resources, such as rooftop solar, batteries, EVs, and flexible demand, create new challenges for voltage management, transformer loading, feeder capacity, and protection settings. Additionally, AI-based tools are increasingly used to support grid operations, emphasizing the need for robust data and models to ensure their performance.

These challenges underscore the necessity for innovative solutions to modernize and enhance the efficiency of electricity grids, addressing issues related to demand growth, thermal limitations, and the integration of emerging technologies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These sectors vary significantly in terms of investment size and the predominant risks faced by investors.
