                              KEY FINDINGS
================================================================================

OVERALL PERFORMANCE:
  • Calibration Accuracy: 70% (19/27 questions)
  • Content Fully Correct: 67% (18/27 questions)
  • Citation Correctness: 76% supported; 24% incorrect
  • Evidence Coverage: 91% (uses most retrieved material)

THE GOOD:
  ✅ When evidence exists, the system uses it well (91% coverage)
  ✅ Answers are correct 100% of the time when evidence is sufficient (14/14)
  ✅ Simple, single-source questions work well (83% accuracy)

THE BAD:
  ❌ Over-answers when evidence is insufficient (56% answer when should abstain)
  ❌ Citations break in multi-source questions (78% error in dependent-source)
  ❌ Struggles with partial evidence (only 25% handle correctly)

THE PRIMARY PROBLEM:
  The system doesn't know when to say "I don't know."
  • When evidence is absent: only 44% abstain (should be 100%)
  • When evidence is partial: only 25% handle correctly
  → This is the biggest opportunity for improvement

Q: Are these numbers real or estimated?

Q: Why does it say "single reviewer"?

Q: What about those three gold corrections (Q10, Q18, Q22)?
A: They were found AFTER the run completed. They're documented separately in

   shows impact of corrections.

Q: Can I modify the frozen files?
A: No. They're marked (FROZEN) because they define the experiment. Results
   come from them; changing them invalidates the whole analysis.

Q: How confident should I be in these results?
A: High on what was measured (evidence sufficiency, citation patterns).
   Lower on generalization (only 27 questions, single model, single reviewer).
   See Limitations section in reports.

Q: What's the most important finding?
A: The system doesn't know when to stop. It answers when it should abstain.
   This is the primary failure mode (56% over-answer on insufficient evidence).

Q: Is this system "good" or "reliable"?
A: No. 70% calibration accuracy and 24% citation errors are significant.
   The system works well on simple, single-source questions (83%) but fails
   on complex multi-source questions (78% citation errors) and partial evidence.
