ℹ️ About This System

EcoRAG is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system trained on environmental sustainability documents. It combines semantic search (BGE embeddings), vector retrieval (Chroma), and language generation (Qwen2.5) to answer questions about green energy, climate action, and environmental policy.

Experiment C evaluates the system's ability to ground answers in retrieved evidence and provide correct citations.
